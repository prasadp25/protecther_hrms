Should a leaver’s final settlement pay a lump-sum gratuity or bonus?

Live now No lump sum. Your salary structure already pays gratuity (4.81%) and statutory bonus (8.33%) every month inside CTC, so the settlement does not pay them again at exit — it shows the statutory figures for reference only. Resignation Approval Workflow

5 decisions
B1 Blocking

When a resignation is approved, does the employee stay on payroll until their last day?

Live now Yes. They stay active — drawing salary and keeping portal access — through the notice period. A manual “Relieve” on or after the last working day marks them Resigned and ends payroll. This is the standard, correct behaviour.
Alternative (not recommended) Mark them Resigned immediately at approval — which would cut off salary and portal access during the notice period.
Confirm — active until last day, then relieve Change:
